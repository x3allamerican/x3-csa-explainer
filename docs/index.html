Why is my CSA score what it is?

Enter your roadside inspections and the severity weight of each violation (shown on your SMS carrier page). See each BASIC measure, which inspections drive it, and when they stop counting. Runs in your browser.

Inspections

Measures

    Follows FMCSA's SMS Methodology (version 3.21, June 2026): time weights 3/2/1 for 0-6, 6-12 and 12-24 months; +2 for out-of-service; 30-point cap per inspection. Percentiles need FMCSA's national peer data and are not computed. FMCSA has approved a revised SMS that is not live yet. Not legal advice.

    Free and open source (MIT) from X3 Fleet Safety: hours of service, driver files, drug and alcohol and CSA in one place.
